
# CA Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The CA Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive sales analytics project designed to provide a clear overview of sales performance across different provinces, products, manufacturers, and categories.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify the highest-performing provinces.
- Analyze revenue by manufacturer.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Analyze units sold across different categories.
- Track revenue trends over time.
- Present key performance indicators through an interactive dashboard.

## Key KPIs

- Total Revenue: $34.12M
- Units Sold: 77.61K
- Available Products: 1.38K

## Key Insights

### Revenue by Province

- Ontario generated the highest revenue at approximately $10.89M.
- Alberta followed with approximately $9.84M.
- Ontario and Alberta represent a significant share of the overall revenue.

### Revenue by Manufacturer

- VanArsdel generated the highest revenue at approximately $14.15M.
- Natura generated approximately $6.54M.
- Aligui generated approximately $3.89M.

### Top Products

The dashboard highlights the top products based on units sold.

Some of the leading products include:

- Currus UE-15
- Currus UE-05
- Other high-performing products from Maximus and Natura

### Units by Category

The dashboard compares units sold across different categories.

Urban and Rural account for the largest share of unit sales.

## Data Preparation

The data was prepared and transformed using Power Query.

The preparation process included:

1. Loading the raw sales data.
2. Cleaning and transforming the dataset.
3. Checking data types and data quality.
4. Preparing fields for analysis.
5. Creating calculated measures using DAX.
6. Building the final interactive dashboard.

## Business Value

This dashboard provides an overview of sales performance and helps users:

- Understand where revenue is generated.
- Identify high-performing products.
- Compare manufacturer performance.
- Analyze category performance.
- Monitor revenue trends.
- Identify areas that may require further analysis.
